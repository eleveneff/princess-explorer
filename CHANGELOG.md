# Changelog

## 5.2.1

- Standing on the garden circle and pressing E, or tapping Play, starts the garden mini-game. Other circles still use E for their own action.

## 5.2

- On foot the drawbridge stays down, in one fixed position. It does not rise when she walks up to it, crosses it, or wanders off. It rises and lowers only while she is in the swan boat, so the boat can pass, and then it stays down.
- The castle gate and the lowered drawbridge use a high three-quarter view along the bridge. Bridge planks and rails that cross the princess fade, so the deck no longer fills the foreground.
- The swan boat camera sits beside and above the boat, clamped over the open water. A building between the lens and the boat fades out.
- Along the stream, tree canopies and columns that graze the camera-to-feet ray fade out. Fence posts and rails are split out of the ground mesh, so one rail can fade without hiding the yard.
- Nameplates and doorway signs turn to face the camera. The library balcony has its own tiled floor. Each stair tread gives a small bounce and a soft step, and the step stays silent when sound is off.
- Saves, coins, stars, items, prices, and the 5.1 migration are unchanged.
- The Royal Garden keeps its beds: a planted, growing, or blooming flower is still there after a reload, and a harvested bed stays empty. The Play and Back buttons stay on the card on a desktop window and on a phone. A missed gate question hides the gate while the lesson and its practice question are up, and that lesson matches the kind of question (comparing, even numbers, or equal groups).
- Two "Planted!" labels near each other step aside instead of sitting on the same spot, and they fade quickly. Garden confetti is a short burst around her, not a screen full of stars.

## 5.1

- Castle rooms are about 1.9 times taller. Walls, doorways, windows, stairs, and decor scale with the new ceiling. Floor surfaces and walk colliders stay on the same plan, and only the castle's own colliders apply indoors.
- The castle camera sits a little higher and further back, stays under the ceiling of the floor she is on, pulls in when a wall is in the way, and fades anything between the lens and the princess. Upper floors fade out while she is below them.
- The garden mini-game and the Garden Terrace use the same idea: the camera pulls in, and hedges, trellises, trees, and props between the camera and the princess fade out.
- Room signs read correctly from both sides. A stair climbs from the library to the balcony garden, and posts or signs on the sightline fade out. The terrace ring has a gap in the low fence so the rail does not cross her feet.
- Saves, coins, stars, items, and prices are unchanged.

## 5.0

- The live game is labeled Princess Explorer 5.0: Learn & Build. Save data still uses the same key.
